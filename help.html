Help & How to Play

IGame01
A 100-round dodge-and-shoot arcade game

Pilot your cute cyan ship, dodge falling enemies, collect gifts, and defeat the boss each round. Clear all 100 rounds to win.

Goal

  • Survive the wave and prepare for the boss (appears after about 30 seconds).
  • Survive contact with enemies and enemy bullets.
  • Beat the round boss to clear the round and unlock the next one.
  • Chase a high score — your best score and cleared rounds are saved on the device.

Controls

Control What it does
Joystick (bottom left) Move your ship in any direction — left, right, up, and down.
FIRE (bottom right) Hold to shoot continuously.
Pause (II) Pause or resume the game.
Sound (SND) Turn sound on or off. Highlighted briefly at the start of each round.
Tap to start On the title screen, tap anywhere to begin (or continue). A 3–2–1–GO countdown plays before combat.

Tip: Keep one thumb on the joystick and the other on FIRE. You can reposition during the countdown; shooting unlocks on GO.

HUD

  • Score / Best — current score and your saved high score.
  • Round X/100 — which round you are on.
  • Boss in Xs — countdown until the boss appears (about 30 seconds). Switches to BOSS FIGHT during the fight.
  • BULLET — shot power level (number at the top). Higher = stronger fire.
  • SHIELD — shield stacks left (number at the top). Each hit spends one stack and softens damage.
  • Weapon — your current shot style.
  • Combo HOLD — combo stays active until you get hit.
  • HP bar — your ship health above the player.

How a round works

  1. A 3–2–1–GO countdown plays (also at the start of each new round).
  2. A short banner shows the round name and the current enemy attack pattern.
  3. Gifts drop first, then enemies fall from the top. Shoot them and dodge their bodies and bullets.
  4. After about 30 seconds, a tough boss appears.
  5. Defeat the boss to clear the round. You heal a bit before the next round starts.
  6. After every 5 cleared rounds, a full-screen ad may appear, then the next round continues.

Enemy attack patterns

Formation layouts change every five rounds (1–5, 6–10, 11–15, and so on). The banner shows the pattern name so you know what to expect:

  • Wide Line, Column Drop, V Sweep, Diagonal
  • Twin Lane, Checker, Crossfire, Sine Wave
  • Pincer, Cluster — then the list repeats

Later enemies can chase your ship, dive faster when close, and shoot back. Shields / tanks are tougher and fire heavier shots.

Weapons & gifts

Fly near falling gifts to collect them — you do not need to touch them exactly. Gifts unlock stronger shot styles and add a BULLET / SHIELD stack (shown as numbers at the top of the screen).

Weapon Style
Blaster Reliable starter shot.
Laser Fast, piercing-style fire.
Spread Wide multi-shot coverage.
Plasma Heavy damage with splash impact.

You always start a session at base Blaster with 0 BULLET / SHIELD stacks. Upgrades come only from gifts during play. When you get hit with shield stacks remaining, you lose 1 stack and take reduced damage.

Combo & power

  • Destroying enemies builds a combo.
  • Combo does not time out on its own — it holds until an enemy or enemy bullet hits you.
  • Higher combo multiplies score and boosts the BULLET number.
  • Gift stacks raise both BULLET (shot power) and SHIELD (damage soak).

Tip: Protect your combo and your shield stacks. Sweep near gifts early in the round before the boss timer runs out.

Progress & save data

  • Cleared rounds are saved, so restarting continues from the next unlocked round.
  • High score and sound mute preference are also saved on your device.
  • Uninstalling the app clears this local progress.

Ads

  • A banner ad stays at the bottom while you play.
  • A full-screen ad can show after every 5th cleared round (5, 10, 15, …).
  • If an ad is not ready, the game continues so you are not stuck.

Quick tips

  • Stay near the middle-bottom so you have room to dodge left and right.
  • Prioritize tanks and dense formations before they fill the screen.
  • Fly near gifts early — stacks matter a lot once the boss arrives.
  • Watch the Boss timer and BULLET / SHIELD numbers at the top.
  • During boss fights, keep moving — each boss shoots a different dense pattern.
  • Use Pause if you need a break. Your progress stays until you quit the app.
