Privacy Policy

IGame01
Effective date: 5 October 2026

This policy describes how IGame01 (“the App”) handles information. The App is a casual action game for iPhone and iPad. It does not require an account and does not ask you to type a name, email address, or password.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves only local game settings on your device using Apple’s standard app storage:

  • High score
  • Highest round you have cleared
  • Sound on/off preference

This data stays on your device. We do not operate our own servers that receive this gameplay data. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

2. Advertising

The App shows ads from Google AdMob, including a banner at the bottom of the screen and full-screen ads after every five completed rounds.

Google and its partners may collect or receive information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (for example IDFA), if you allow tracking
  • IP address, device type, operating system, and general location derived from IP
  • Ad views, clicks, and similar advertising events
  • Consent choices you make in privacy forms

We use this advertising to keep the App free. We do not sell your personal information. Google’s advertising practices are described here: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can opt out of personalized ads in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and through Google’s ad settings: https://adssettings.google.com.

3. Consent and tracking

Where required by law (including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the App shows Google’s User Messaging Platform consent form before requesting personalized ads.

On Apple devices, the App may also show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt (“Allow Tracking?”). If you decline, the App can still run and may still show non-personalized or limited ads.

Apple may also receive SKAdNetwork conversion information used to measure ad campaigns without sharing your identity with us.

4. Permissions the App does not use

The App does not request access to:

  • Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Precise GPS location
  • Health, HomeKit, or payment card details

5. Children

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through ads, contact us using the details below so we can help you use Google’s and Apple’s tools.

6. Data retention

Local game data remains until you delete the App or reset it on the device. Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies.

7. International processing

Advertising partners may process data in the United States and other countries. Those countries may have different data-protection laws than your own.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal data, and to withdraw consent. For information held only on your device, you can delete the App. For advertising data, use the Google and Apple controls linked above, or contact us and we will point you to the relevant partner.

9. Changes

We may update this policy if the App’s features or advertising setup changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when that happens.
